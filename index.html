Our services

We provide four principal services, which may be engaged individually or together.

Accounts and tax returns

Annual accounts and the associated tax returns, prepared from your records and filed with HMRC and, for limited companies, Companies House. This keeps your filing obligations up to date and gives you a clear record of your financial position.

What the service includes
  • Annual accounts for sole traders, partnerships and limited companies
  • Company Tax Returns and Corporation Tax computations
  • Self Assessment tax returns for sole traders, landlords and directors
  • Submission to HMRC and Companies House
Who it is suitable for

Sole traders, partnerships and limited companies that need annual accounts, and landlords and company directors who need a Self Assessment tax return.

What we need from you

Your bank statements and your sales and purchase records, or access to your accounting software, together with your tax reference numbers and your most recent accounts and tax returns where available.

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, please contact us.

Ask a question
Working with usHow do I become a client?

There are four stages. You contact us by telephone, email or the enquiry form. We confirm the scope of our work and our fee in an engagement letter. We verify your identity, as the Money Laundering Regulations require. We then obtain your records and begin the work.

Working with usCan you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. With your authority, we write to your current accountant to request professional clearance, together with the information we need to take over your affairs, such as your records and your most recent accounts and tax returns.

Professional clearance confirms that there is no professional reason why we should not act for you. You do not need to manage the handover yourself.

Working with usWhat information do you require to begin?

Photographic identification and proof of address for identity verification, your tax reference numbers, and your most recent accounts and tax returns where available. For ongoing work we also require your bank statements and sales and purchase records, or access to your accounting software.

We will also ask you to authorise us as your agent, so that we may deal with HMRC on your behalf.

Working with usWhat are your fees?

Our fees depend on the work involved: a single tax return costs less than a year of bookkeeping, VAT and payroll. Tell us what you require and we will confirm our fee in writing before any work begins.

Working with usDo you act for clients outside Bury and Manchester?

Yes. We are based in Bury, Manchester, and act for businesses and individuals throughout the United Kingdom. You do not need to be local to us.

Working with usDoes a sole trader need an accountant?

You may prepare and file your own tax return, and many sole traders do. An accountant is of most value when your affairs demand more time than you have available, when you are uncertain which expenses may be claimed, or when an error or a missed deadline would result in a penalty.

Once a sole trader’s qualifying income is above the Making Tax Digital threshold, quarterly updates are required in addition to the annual return, and it is often at this point that an accountant is appointed.
